This Privacy Policy explains how BookByte handles personal information when you visit our pages or purchase our digital e-books. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to process orders, deliver products and provide customer support.

1. Information We May Receive

When you place an order, we may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, order details and payment status. Payment card, UPI or banking credentials are handled by the payment provider; please do not send those credentials to us.

2. How We Use Information

We may use order and contact information to process and confirm purchases, deliver digital e-books, respond to questions, provide support, resolve payment or delivery issues, maintain transaction records and meet applicable legal requirements.

3. Payments

Payments may be processed through Razorpay. Your payment is subject to Razorpay's applicable terms and privacy policy. We may receive transaction-related information, such as payment confirmation and an order or transaction reference, to verify and fulfil your purchase.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers, including payment and delivery providers, when needed to complete your order or provide support. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for order fulfilment, customer support, record-keeping and legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Third-Party Services

Our checkout or other services may be provided by third-party platforms. Those platforms handle information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review their policies before providing information to them.

7. Your Choices and Requests

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your order or request a correction, subject to applicable legal and record-keeping requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Our e-books are intended to be purchased by adults. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.
